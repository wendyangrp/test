Empathy Toolkit

Design thinking · Empathy stage

Tools for the empathy stage of design thinking

The empathy stage is about understanding the people you design for: what they do, think, feel and struggle with. These small tools help you practise that before (and alongside) talking to real users.

Tools

  • Persona Interview

    Describe a persona, then interview them. The persona answers in character, so you can practise asking good, open questions.

    Tell me about the last time you felt lost in a lecture.
    Honestly? Week two. Everyone else seemed to follow, so I just copied the board.
